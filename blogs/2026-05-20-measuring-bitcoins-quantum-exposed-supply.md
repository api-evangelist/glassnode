Research mapping Bitcoin's public-key exposure finds 6.04M BTC (30.2% of supply) shows at-rest exposure, split between 1.92M BTC of structural exposure from script types like P2PK and Taproot, and 4.12M BTC of operational exposure from address reuse, providing a baseline map for address-hygiene mitigation.
