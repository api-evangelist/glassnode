Treasuries out-yield the crypto carry trade for only the second time on record, and the downstream surfaces have gone quiet with it: spot volume at its lowest since 2019, exchange flows becalmed, the offer side of the book thinned out. The drawdown is shallow, and short of its predecessors' clock.
